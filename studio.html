What is this?

A unified platform for scientific workflow execution

Research institutions worldwide have depended on Pegasus WMS for over two decades to orchestrate large-scale scientific workflows — from gravitational wave detection and genome sequencing to climate modeling and particle physics.

PegasusAI Studio brings that power to the browser: describe your workflow in natural language, let the AI generate and configure it, then execute it across hundreds or thousands of cores with a single click.

How it works

From research goal to running workflow in three steps

1 Define your workflow

Describe your scientific goal in plain language. The AI translates your intent into a fully configured Pegasus WMS workflow — complete with scripts, dependencies, and execution settings.

2 Submit with one click

Your workflow is compiled and dispatched automatically — running on your local cluster, your institution's computing infrastructure, or distributed across global shared pools.

3 Monitor in real time

Track every job as it executes across your workflow. The AI surfaces failures, diagnoses root causes, and recommends corrective actions before issues cascade.

Features

Capabilities built for research at scale

01

AI Assistant

Natural language workflow authoring

  • Compose workflows by describing your scientific goals
  • Diagnose failures with AI-powered root-cause analysis
  • Auto-generate execution scripts and configuration files
  • Expert Pegasus WMS guidance without reading documentation
02

Live Dashboard

Complete workflow observability

  • Interactive DAG visualization of your full workflow graph
  • Per-job execution status updated in real time
  • One-click access to logs, stderr output, and exit codes
  • Proactive alerts when jobs stall, fail, or exceed resource limits
03

Notebooks

Integrated computational environment

  • Browser-based Jupyter notebooks with zero configuration
  • Immediate access to your workflow inputs, outputs, and data
  • Python, R, and Julia kernels available on demand
  • AI assistant embedded alongside your code for in-context help
04

Compute Power

Elastic infrastructure at any scale

  • Execute workflows on your local or institutional computing cluster
  • Seamlessly burst to OSPool's 19,000+ cores across global sites
  • Real-time resource utilization and quota visibility
  • Threshold alerts before capacity limits are reached
