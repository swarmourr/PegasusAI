Research Areas

Technical Approach

Five integrated research threads that together make Pegasus workflows faster, smarter, and more accessible.

01

Task Performance Prediction

ML models that predict task runtime, memory usage, and resource needs to enable smarter scheduling decisions.
02

Comprehensive Online Monitoring

Real-time performance tracking that detects anomalies, surfaces bottlenecks, and supports live workflow introspection.
03

AI-Assisted Workflow Creation

LLM-powered tools that help scientists compose, validate, and refine scientific workflows through natural language.
04

PegasusAI in the Classroom

Educational integration of Pegasus AI into academic curricula, enabling students to engage with real-world scientific computing.
05

CI-Ready Design

Implemented within Pegasus's modular architecture to ensure compatibility with the NSF cyberinfrastructure ecosystem.
