Pegasus AI Studio — Available Now

Science at scale,
in your browser

Studio is the browser-based interface to Pegasus AI — describe your pipeline in plain language, let the AI build and configure it, then execute it across hundreds or thousands of cores with a single click.

Try Studio Learn more
  • AI workflow authoring

    Describe your scientific goal — the AI generates a fully configured Pegasus workflow.

  • Real-time DAG visualization

    Inspect and monitor your workflow graph as jobs run across the distributed infrastructure.

  • One-click execution

    Submit to OSPool, ACCESS allocations, or your institution's cluster without touching a terminal.

  • Integrated Jupyter notebooks

    Analyze results, build scripts, and iterate — all within the same AI-assisted environment.
